Services & Sample Pricing

Structured financial management designed specifically for independent businesses, creative practices, and growing ventures in Oregon.

Foundation

Bookkeeping & Account Reconciliation

Systematic monthly recording and classification of transactions to keep your general ledger balanced and accurate.

Who It Is For

Sole proprietors, independent consultancies, creative agencies, and small retail operations seeking to replace messy spreadsheets with an orderly monthly routine.

What Is Included

  • Monthly bank, credit card, and merchant statement reconciliations
  • Income statement (P&L) and balance sheet delivery by the 15th of each month
  • Expense ledger classification and digital receipt organization
  • Year-end Form 1099 vendor payment summary preparation
Payroll & State Rules

Payroll Support & Compliance

Administrative coordination of employee wage runs, state withholdings, and payroll ledger integration.

Who It Is For

Owner-operated S-Corporations managing reasonable officer compensation, and small employers with salaried or hourly team members in Oregon.

What Is Included

  • Payroll software setup and run review (e.g., Gusto, QuickBooks Payroll)
  • Tracking of Oregon Paid Leave and statewide transit tax withholdings
  • Monthly payroll journal entries and wage liability reconciliations
  • Annual Form W-2 reconciliation verification
Compliance Filings

Tax Preparation & Filing Support

Meticulous federal, state, and local business tax return preparation compiled from verified documentation.

Who It Is For

Sole proprietorships, single-member LLCs, partnerships, and S-Corporations needing compliant annual returns and calculated quarterly estimated vouchers.

What Is Included

  • Federal business returns (Schedule C, Form 1065, or Form 1120-S)
  • Oregon State and City of Portland / Multnomah County Business Income Tax filings
  • Quarterly estimated tax calculations and calendar reminders
  • Fixed asset and depreciation schedule management

Note: Tax filing results are determined by documented revenue, allowable deductions, and applicable tax regulations. We do not provide guarantees regarding refunds or audit outcomes.

Advisory

Small Business Advisory

Financial review and analysis to help business owners make grounded operational decisions.

Who It Is For

Established founders navigating staffing changes, seasonal revenue variations, equipment investments, or cash flow planning.

What Is Included

  • 12-month rolling cash flow modeling and runway estimates
  • Operating expense review to examine overhead cost trends
  • Quarterly structured strategic review video meetings
  • Budget-to-actual variance reporting with plain-language notes
