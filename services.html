Services & Sample Pricing

Structured financial management designed specifically for independent businesses, creative practices, and growing ventures in Oregon.

Core Foundation

Bookkeeping & Account Reconciliation

Reliable monthly recording and classification of transactions to keep your general ledger balanced, organized, and ready for reporting.

Who It Is For

Sole proprietors, independent consultancies, creative studios, and small retail operations seeking to replace disorganized spreadsheets with an orderly, monthly cloud bookkeeping routine.

What Is Included

  • Monthly bank, credit card, and loan statement reconciliations
  • Income statement (P&L) and balance sheet delivery by the 15th
  • Digital receipt categorization and expense ledger management
  • Accounts payable and accounts receivable tracking support
  • Annual Form 1099 vendor payment summary preparation
Team & Compliance

Payroll Support & Oregon Compliance

Administrative coordination of employee wage runs, state withholdings, and payroll system record keeping.

Who It Is For

Owner-operated S-Corporations with officer compensation, and small local employers managing 1 to 15 salaried or hourly team members in Oregon.

What Is Included

  • Payroll platform setup and maintenance (e.g., Gusto, QuickBooks Payroll)
  • Tracking of Oregon Paid Leave and statewide transit tax requirements
  • Monthly payroll ledger journal entry creation and reconciliation
  • New-hire onboarding paperwork checklist support (W-4, I-9)
  • Year-end payroll summary and Form W-2 reconciliation verification
Annual Compliance

Tax Preparation & Filing Support

Meticulous federal, state, and local tax return preparation compiled from verified financial documentation.

Who It Is For

Sole proprietorships, single-member LLCs, general partnerships, and S-Corporations that need accurate, compliant annual tax filings and calculated quarterly estimates.

What Is Included

  • Federal business returns (Schedule C, Form 1065, or Form 1120-S)
  • Oregon State and City of Portland / Multnomah County business filings
  • Quarterly estimated tax voucher calculations and payment reminder dates
  • Depreciation schedule maintenance and capital asset categorization
  • Secure digital archiving of past filed returns for your records

Note: Tax filing results are determined by your documented revenue, deductible expenses, and applicable statutes. We do not provide guarantees regarding tax savings, audit outcomes, or refunds.

Strategic Growth

Small Business Advisory

Objective financial analysis and planning to help founders make clear-headed decisions regarding cash, margins, and operational spending.

Who It Is For

Established business owners navigating staffing transitions, seasonal revenue shifts, equipment purchases, or seeking structured monthly cash flow guidance.

What Is Included

  • 12-month rolling cash flow modeling and runway tracking
  • Operating expense review to identify cost trends and overhead bloat
  • Quarterly structured strategic review video conferences
  • Key performance indicator (KPI) metric tracking and summary dashboards
  • Budget-to-actual variance reporting with plain-language notes
