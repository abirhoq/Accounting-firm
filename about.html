Practice Overview

Grounded accounting with a Pacific Northwest perspective.

Harbor & Pine Accounting is a studio concept built around the belief that small business finance should be calm, intelligible, and supportive.

Core Principles

Values that guide our approach

Rather than treating accounting as a once-a-year compliance burden, we approach financial records as a vital tool for clear-headed decision-making.

Clarity in Plain Language

Financial statements should not read like an obscure riddle. We take time to explain reports in straightforward, actionable language so you always understand what your numbers mean.

Deliberate Capacity

We intentionally limit the volume of active monthly client accounts we take on. This allows us to provide thorough, unhurried attention to each client rather than functioning as an impersonal ticket queue.

Meticulous Data Care

Your financial data is handled through modern, secure cloud portals with strict role permissions. We treat every receipt, statement, and tax voucher with methodical confidentiality.

Realistic & Honest Guidance

We do not promise unrealistic tax loopholes or fabricated refund windfalls. We focus on diligent compliance, accurate ledger management, and reliable financial routines.

Client Experience

Our step-by-step onboarding process

Transitioning your books or onboarding a new accounting partner should feel seamless. Here is how we welcome and integrate new clients.

Step 1

Initial Diagnostic

We review your existing software setup, recent tax returns, and general ledger accounts to understand your historical transaction structure.

Step 2

Secure Connection

We set up read-only bank feeds, invite users to our encrypted document portal, and organize digital receipt collection folders.

Step 3

Initial Month Close

We complete the first full month of reconciliations together, resolve any uncategorized items, and review the initial reporting packet.

Step 4

Steady-State Cadence

With the foundation settled, your books are reconciled monthly on an automated timetable, supported by proactive quarterly reviews.
