Practice Concept

Grounded accounting with a Pacific Northwest perspective.

Harbor & Pine Accounting is a studio concept built around the idea that small business finance should be calm, intelligible, and supportive.

Firm Values

Values that guide our approach

Rather than treating accounting as a once-a-year scramble, we view financial records as a practical tool for operational decision-making.

Clarity in Plain Language

Financial reports should not read like an obscure riddle. We explain statements in plain, actionable language so you always understand what your numbers represent.

Deliberate Capacity

We prioritize manageable client rosters so every business receives responsive, unhurried attention instead of being routed through an impersonal support ticket queue.

Methodical Organization

From chart-of-accounts structure to monthly reconciliations, we emphasize structured consistency so records remain clean, balanced, and tax-ready.

Realistic & Honest Guidance

We focus on diligent compliance, accurate ledger management, and steady operational routines without making exaggerated promises.

Client Onboarding

Our step-by-step onboarding process

Transitioning books to a new partner should be structured and predictable. Here is our typical onboarding sequence.

01

Initial Diagnostic

We review your existing software setup, past returns, and general ledger accounts to evaluate historic categorization.

02

Software Access

We connect accountant access to your software and configure bank feeds for ongoing transaction imports.

03

First Month Close

We complete the first full month of reconciliations, resolve unclassified entries, and review the initial reporting packet together.

04

Routine Cadence

Your books are balanced monthly on a fixed timetable, supported by quarterly strategic check-ins.
