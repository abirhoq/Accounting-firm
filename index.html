Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about accounting consulting

A quick overview of what to expect when partnering with an outside accounting advisory practice.

We primarily collaborate with businesses using cloud-based platforms such as QuickBooks Online and Xero. If you are currently working with spreadsheets or a desktop system, we can assist with organizing and transitioning your chart of accounts during the onboarding phase.

We emphasize client privacy and security. Clients utilize encrypted document portals and read-only bank feed integrations. We do not ask clients to email unencrypted spreadsheets containing sensitive tax identification or banking details.

Our core service focuses on ongoing monthly partnerships because continuous reconciliation provides the most reliable financial visibility. However, we also conduct diagnostic record cleanups for new clients whose prior quarters or tax years require catch-up reconciliation.

When your transactions are categorized and reconciled monthly, year-end tax preparation becomes an orderly, predictable process. Your annual return is compiled directly from balanced ledgers, avoiding the delays associated with reconstructing a full year of records in April.

We use transparent, flat-rate monthly pricing tiers based on transaction volume, account quantity, and specific advisory requirements. You can review representative figures on our services page under the sample pricing section.
