Common Inquiries

Frequently asked questions

Key details regarding client communication, software platforms, and engagement structure.

We primarily work with cloud platforms like QuickBooks Online and Xero. If you currently use spreadsheets or desktop software, we assist with initial chart of accounts setup during onboarding.

Clients connect read-only bank feeds directly within their accounting software and submit receipts and invoices via standard cloud storage links.

Our core focus is ongoing monthly support. When taking on new clients who are behind on historical reconciliations, we perform an onboarding catch-up review before starting the regular monthly cycle.

Fees are billed as flat monthly retainers based on monthly transaction volume, account counts, and payroll complexity. You can view representative tiers on our Services & Pricing page.
